Notes

  • All prices on this page are the clinic's prices to patients, before GST, rounded up to the whole dollar. Lab and imaging cost prices are not shown. Combined orders add up each test's sell price. Unpriced Innoquest panels stay price on request.
  • Pathlab tests are from the Pathlab Singapore 2025 service catalogue (version 24 Jul 2025). Single tests are from pp.133–143; tests marked # run on set days (p.144). Send-out tests (toxicology, stool cultures, vaginal PCR profiles VP1–VP5) have no printed price: call Pathlab on 6742 9011.
  • Innoquest tests are from its printed 2026 service catalogue, including the single-test list (pp.211–231).
  • Single tests: search a test name or code in All panels, or pick “Single tests”. In the diagnosis view, options at either lab may combine a panel with single tests, or use single tests alone, when that is cheaper.
  • Allergy tests are ImmunoCAP specific IgE. Codes such as F13 (peanut) or D1 (house dust mite) are the allergen codes; order by Innoquest's order code (e.g. A24). Turnaround is 2–5 working days. Pathlab offers fixed allergy screens instead (ASTA, 54 allergens, and ASTC, 62 allergens), run on Wednesdays.
  • Pathlab MCED (Cancer ScreenDx by AGTC Genomics, run in Malaysia): prices use the promotion valid until 31 Dec 2026. Comprehensive MCED orders come with a free SG60 profile (worth S$400 at patient price), and Women's/Men's MCED with a free SG50 profile (S$280). Basic MCED has no free profile. Their contents (Pathlab Appendix A) are on the SG50 and SG60 cards in the MCED category. SG60 adds ESR, magnesium, vitamin D, CA 19-9, EBV, H. pylori, ApoA1/ApoB, progesterone or testosterone and a stool OB test with immuno reflex. Both are fasting profiles; SG60 also needs a stool sample. These are listed for reference only and aren't used when the page works out the cheapest option.
  • Lucence tests are from its 1 Oct 2026 price list (test only; sample handling extra), ordered online.
  • MCED is a screening aid, not a diagnosis. A negative result does not rule out cancer, so keep routine screening going. A positive result needs confirmatory imaging or biopsy. The MCED codes on this page are labels for this reference, not lab order codes.
  • RadLink prices are from its January 2026 price list. Exams marked “+ contrast” need IV contrast added (MRI S$360, CT S$200). Prices exclude non-standard contrast and supplies. 3D mammogram and bone densitometry are at Paragon only. PET scans marked with a deposit need it paid before booking. For exams not listed, RadLink quotes on request.
  • AsiaMedic X-rays are from its radiology price list effective 1 Jan 2026. Add-on charges (extra view, duplicate report or DVD, film copies) are flat fees. AsiaMedic notes prices can change without notice.
  • Diagnosis suggestions are a starting point. They follow common primary-care workups and do not replace clinical judgement or current MOH guidance.
  • Scheduled tests (marked #) run on set days, so results take longer (catalogue p.144).
  • HIV: a positive screen is confirmed at the National HIV Reference Laboratory, SGH. Innoquest HIV results can take up to 14 working days if confirmation is needed.
